# PLACE@ASET — Student Quick Start Guide
### *Your First 30 Minutes to Placement Readiness*

Welcome to **PLACE@ASET**! This guide takes you from your first login to your first completed practice session and readiness evaluation in under 30 minutes.

---

## ⏱️ The 30-Minute Roadmap

```
[00:00 - 05:00] Step 1: Sign In & Verify Profile
       ↓
[05:00 - 10:00] Step 2: Understand Your Command Center & "Your Next Step"
       ↓
[10:00 - 22:00] Step 3: Complete Your First 10-Question Practice Set
       ↓
[22:00 - 26:00] Step 4: Inspect Your Diagnostic Readiness & Analytics
       ↓
[26:00 - 30:00] Step 5: Explore Your Personal Learning Studio
```

---

## Step 1: Sign In & Complete Profile (5 Mins)

1. Navigate to your college portal URL and log in with your institutional email.
2. If prompted, visit **Account Settings** (`/settings`):
   - Confirm your **Department** and **Roll Number**.
   - Add 2–3 target dream companies (e.g., *Amazon*, *TCS Digital*, *Accenture*).
   - Click **Save Changes**.

> **💡 Why this matters**: The recommendation engine uses your department and target recruiters to customize your practice questions and company eligibility metrics.

---

## Step 2: Understand Your Command Center (5 Mins)

When you land on `/dashboard`, notice the clean, intuitive layout:

1. **Focus Command Card (Top)**:
   - Displays your daily streak counter and daily goal target (default: 5 questions).
2. **"Your Next Step" Card (Directly Below)**:
   - Answers the single question: *"What should I do right now?"*
   - Gives you one dominant action with clear explanation and zero technical jargon.
3. **Key Metric Tiles**:
   - Track your solved count, active challenges, college rank, and placement readiness index.
4. **Grouped Navigation Sidebar**:
   - **LEARN**: Dashboard, Practice Arena, Resource Hub, AI Mentor, Personal Studio.
   - **PROGRESS**: Placement Readiness, AI Analytics, Leaderboard.
   - **CONNECT**: Community, Calendar & Deadlines, Weekly Challenges.
   - **CAREER**: AI Resume Builder, Interview Simulator, Credentials & Badges.

---

## Step 3: Complete Your First Practice Session (12 Mins)

Click the **Start Your First Practice** button on your dashboard, or select **Practice Arena** (`/practice`) in the sidebar:

1. **Select a Mode**:
   - Choose **Technical Practice** (Data Structures, DBMS, OS) or **Quantitative Aptitude**.
2. **Select Difficulty**:
   - Start with **Medium** or **Easy** to calibrate your initial benchmark.
3. **Choose Question Count**:
   - Pick **10 Questions**.
4. **Start Session**:
   - Click **Start Arena Session**.
5. **Answer with Evaluation**:
   - Read each question, choose an option, and submit.
   - Read the detailed explanation and time breakdown.
   - Bookmark any tricky questions using the 🔖 icon.
6. **Review Results**:
   - View your accuracy percentage, earned XP points, and speed statistics upon submission.

---

## Step 4: Check Your Placement Readiness (4 Mins)

Now that you have completed at least one practice session:

1. Navigate to **Placement Readiness** (`/dashboard/readiness`):
   - Notice how your **Placement Readiness Diagnostic** has updated from 0 to your real calibrated score.
   - Review your **Topic Proficiency** radar: your highest and lowest scoring areas.
   - Review the **Company Eligibility Matrix** to see which companies match your performance tier.
2. Return to the **Dashboard** (`/dashboard`):
   - Notice how **"Your Next Step"** has dynamically shifted! It now highlights your weakest topic (e.g., *"Review DBMS & SQL — your recent accuracy is 40%"*) with a direct practice button.

---

## Step 5: Explore Your Personal Learning Studio (4 Mins)

1. Open **Personal Studio** (`/personal`) from the sidebar.
2. Click **Upload Notes / Material**.
3. Paste text from a recent lecture, class notes, or copy a topic from Wikipedia.
4. Click **Create Personal Document**.
5. Watch the AI instantly generate:
   - An executive summary of the concepts.
   - Interactive flashcards to test your recall.
   - An instant quiz generated directly from your own text!

---

## 🎉 You're All Set!

You are now officially up and running on PLACE@ASET. 

**Pro-Tips for Daily Success:**
- ⚡ **Solve 5 questions a day** to maintain your streak and climb the campus leaderboard.
- 🎯 **Follow "Your Next Step"**: It automatically detects your weakest areas and prepares you for upcoming college deadlines.
- 💬 **Ask the AI Mentor (`/mentor`)** whenever you get stuck on an algorithm or system concept.
